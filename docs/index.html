Balance sheet groups

Order, name and section of each group. Every statement page, the cash flow, the exports and the reports follow this page. The cash-flow setting applies to every account in the group; single accounts can deviate in the chart of accounts, and Statement settings can override it per account.

Income statement groups

A running subtotal follows the group and adds up from the top. A block total adds the groups since the previous subtotal. Leave empty for none. Subtotals named Total income, Gross result, EBITDA, EBIT and EBT are recognised by the overview and the key figures.

Cash flow classes

Where the cash of each balance sheet account goes in the cash flow statement. An account in Cash counts like a bank account; a credit card there counts like one with a negative balance, so card purchases show under their category. Income and expense accounts are always operating; the FX accounts and unrealised value changes are exchange rate effects. Shown under puts the cash of an operating account on an income statement line instead of its own (taxes paid under Direct taxes). Prepayments booked as spreads always show under the accounts the spread releases to. Amounts: cash flow between the two reports chosen on the statement pages.

Investments

Which accounts make up each investment: the pay-in accounts and the account holding its value adjustment.

Years in this file

Each year opens with the closing of the year before: balance sheet accounts carry over, equity and the result roll into the equity-at-beginning account. Nothing is booked for that. A closed year is read-only; reopen it to change a booking.

Imported closings

Closing balance lists from Banana, Abacus, Excel or this program, for years without bookings in this file. Only the balance per account is stored. The closing of the year just before the first booked year is its opening balance.

Comparison file

Another accounting file with all its bookings, for example last year. It is only read, never changed, and evaluated with the structure of this file.
